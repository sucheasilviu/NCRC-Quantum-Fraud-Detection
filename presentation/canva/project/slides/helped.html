What actually helped

Remove every CNOT

Noise stops reordering payments: AUPRC at p = 0.05; the spread more than halves.

0.712 → 0.735

Packed gradients

Shift one angle on all 4 independent qubits at once. Identical results on every seed.

Cheaper training

15 epochs, check progress on 20% of validation. Small AUPRC cost.

0.735 → 0.721

Optional ranking loss

Reward frauds scored above legitimate payments. AUPRC up on 3 of 5 seeds.

F1 0.763 → 0.796

All on the full test set, 5 seeds · runs = circuit runs per training payment

Speaking time: 2:55 to 3:45. SAY: We tested each change separately. Removing the CNOTs is the core: the same circuit with CNOTs drops to 0.712 at strong noise and varies twice as much; without them it holds 0.735. Because the four qubits never interact, one circuit run can shift the same angle on all four and still read each qubit’s own gradient: 15 runs per training payment instead of 81, with mathematically identical results; we re-trained all five seeds to check. Cheap training, 15 epochs and checking progress on 20% of validation, costs 0.014 AUPRC. An optional ranking term in the loss lifts F1 to 0.796 on every seed, but AUPRC only on three of five, so we keep the plain version as the headline. Source: results/raw, RESULTS.md.
