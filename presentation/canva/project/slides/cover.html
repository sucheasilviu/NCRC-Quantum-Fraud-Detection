Fraud detection under noise

Our VQC ignores the noise.

It ranks fraud above logistic regression.

Noise-Covariant Re-uploading Classifier · 5 seeds · 56,962 test payments, 98 frauds

Speaking time: 0:00 to 0:15. SAY: We built a quantum fraud detector whose ranking of payments does not change at all under the challenge’s gate noise.
